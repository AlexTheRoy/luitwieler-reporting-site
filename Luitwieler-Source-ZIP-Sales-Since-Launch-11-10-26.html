The short read. GOAL sent 14,844 leads from Oct 1, 2024 to Nov 10, 2026, spread across 1,320 ZIP codes and 57 counties. Riverside and San Bernardino counties have the most sales, 51 each, and Sacramento and the Central Valley sent the most leads, 4,182, with 125 sales. Orange County sent 1,629 leads with 28 sales. Premium Referral has the most sales by source (87, across Auto (High Quality) and Auto (Standard)), and Tier 3 on Home the lowest CPA among sources with ten or more sales on the campaigns with spend.

What the data can show. Quotes and sales come from your 39 status files (Dec 10, 2024 to Nov 4, 2026, counting each repeat upload once), matched to GOAL leads by phone and by the lead date where a file has one, plus the statuses set on leads in GOAL. They show 2,121 quoted leads since launch: 2,114 in your files and 7 set to Quoted with Contact in GOAL only. CPA is spend divided by households sold, counted on leads that arrived in the period, on the campaigns with GOAL spend. GOAL's spend report for this account covers Auto (High Quality), Home and the Marketplace. Auto (Standard) and Auto (Partner Network) ran from Oct 2024 to mid 2025 and are no longer running; they are not in that report, so their 5,248 leads are counted from the lead records and their spend is not shown. Every CPA and cost per item on these pages uses the campaigns with spend only. Cost per item is spend divided by items sold. Items come from the Items column of your files (exports and sales reports from May 2026 on): each household counts the Items on its rows in the most recent file with an Items column that lists it. The files are cumulative, so items are never added across files. 110 of the 397 households sold since launch have two or more items. The 167 sales with no item count, most of them 2024 and 2025 leads from before your files carried Items, are counted as 1 item each (32 of them are marked Sold - Multi Policy in GOAL, so the true count is higher). That makes 551 items. 617 phone numbers sit on more than one GOAL lead, from households that asked again; each household counts one sale. Repeat submissions made in the same second are counted once. 7 sales are left out because the household was already a customer: it was marked Sold in a file dated before that lead arrived. Your exports are cumulative, so the latest export that lists a lead decides whether it is sold: 36 leads (all but one from 2025) that were marked Sold in an earlier export show no sale in the latest one, so they are counted as not sold.
